# Worked synthetic example

`financials.csv` is a made-up company (`DEMO`), not actual market data. `analyst.md`, `risk.md`, and `proposal.md` are a complete mock-generated research chain, saved in review before human approval. The PM proposes fifty shares at a hypothetical snapshot price of one hundred dollars, from an initial paper cash balance of one hundred thousand dollars.

The reports retain generated IDs, content hashes, model/mock digests, prompt versions, input dates and parent links to illustrate the real proposal format. They are static documentation and cannot be imported or executed. Follow the README quickstart to create fresh proposals in your own ledger; inspect and approve each separately. Expiry dates in these snapshots are intentionally not extended.

Research and education tool only; not financial advice. Paper trading only.
